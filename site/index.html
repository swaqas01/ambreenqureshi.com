01Meet Ambreen Qureshi

The operational leadership behind Amber Homes.

Ambreen Qureshi is the Founder and Managing Director of Amber Homes Real Estate, an award-winning Dubai brokerage established in 2017. Under her leadership, the company has earned Top Platinum Sales Agency recognition from Dubai Holding, Meraas and Nakheel for four consecutive years.

A gold medalist in Economics, Ambreen has worked in Dubai real estate since 2011, building expertise in property investment, team development and operational excellence. She leads the standards behind Amber Homes, from client care and training to documentation and transaction management.

The name “Amber Homes” is derived from Ambreen, reflecting the personal vision behind the company.

07FAQ

Ambreen Qureshi, in plain answers.

Who is Ambreen Qureshi?

Ambreen Qureshi is the Founder and Managing Director of Amber Homes Real Estate, a RERA-licensed Dubai brokerage founded in 2017 (ORN 18690). She has worked in Dubai real estate since 2011.

What is Ambreen Qureshi’s role at Amber Homes?

She leads the company’s operations, team, processes and client-care standards — the structure behind how enquiries, documentation and transactions are managed as the brokerage grows.

Is Ambreen Qureshi the founder of Amber Homes?

Yes. She is publicly identified as Founder and Managing Director of Amber Homes Real Estate, which she leads together with Founder and Managing Partner Saad Waqas. The company is named after her — “Amber”, from Ambreen.

What recognition has Amber Homes received?

Platinum Agency 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 — Meraas - Nakheel & Dubai Holding. Amber Homes Real Estate is the only company recognised as Platinum Agency for 4 Consecutive Years. Further recognition: Nshama (2020) and Binghatti (2026).

Was Ambreen Qureshi featured by RAKBANK?

Yes. Her entrepreneurial journey was featured through RAKBANK’s “She Means Business” campaign, which highlights female entrepreneurship in the UAE.

What is Amber Homes’ RERA registration?

Amber Homes Real Estate operates under RERA ORN 18690, with its office at Burj Al Salam Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai.

How can someone contact Ambreen Qureshi?

Enquiries can be submitted through the contact form on this website, by telephone on +971 4 368 4497, or through the Amber Homes team on WhatsApp. Client engagements are handled through Amber Homes Real Estate.
