On the 2011 start date

Ambreen entered Dubai real estate in 2011. Some external profiles have carried 2012; where they do, they are being corrected so that every source agrees. The chronology above uses 2011 throughout, and the correction is logged on the Facts & Sources page.

Company milestones versus personal ones

The awards in this chronology were earned by Amber Homes Real Estate as a company; Ambreen’s part in them is set out on the Awards & Recognition page. Personal recognition — the RAKBANK feature — is marked as such. The distinction is kept deliberately, because collapsing the two is how personal-brand sites lose credibility.
