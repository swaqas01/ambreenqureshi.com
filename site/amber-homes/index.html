Home  /  Amber Homes

The company she built and continues to lead.

Amber Homes Real Estate is a RERA-licensed Dubai brokerage founded in 2017 — named “Amber”, from Ambreen — operating across off-plan, ready and luxury property.

As Founder and Managing Director, Ambreen oversees the operational structure supporting the company’s advisors, clients and transactions — alongside Founder and Managing Partner Saad Waqas, who leads the company’s investment advisory and market strategy.

Amber Homes operates under RERA ORN 18690 from Burj Al Salam Tower on Sheikh Zayed Road, and has reported more than USD 1.5 billion in cumulative company sales — an internally reported figure. Its standing as a Platinum Agency for Meraas, Nakheel & Dubai Holding for four consecutive years (2022–2025) reflects the consistency of the wider team and its operating standards — recognition from Dubai Holding, Dubai’s largest government-owned developer group.

The wider group

Amber Homes Interiors (2019) extends the company into design and fit-out, and Amber Holiday Homes (2021) — particularly Ambreen’s brainchild — operates short-stay residences in Dubai. Each business runs on the same operating discipline as the brokerage: defined processes, measured service and named ownership of every open item.
