01The four-year Platinum record · 2022–2025

Amber Homes Real Estate received Platinum recognition for four consecutive years, 2022 through 2025, across the Black Onyx Awards associated with Meraas, Nakheel, Dubai Properties and the wider Dubai Holding real-estate ecosystem. The Black Onyx Awards honour Dubai’s highest-performing brokerages on delivered sales performance.

04Evidence & methodology

Award titles on this page are transcribed from the certificates, plaques and official announcements themselves — the 2025 plaque, for example, reads: “Platinum Award, presented to Amber Homes Real Estate L.L.C, in recognition of exceptional performance, dedication, and outstanding accomplishment in achieving excellence within Dubai’s real estate community”, presented by Meraas and Nakheel. Where a photograph is not yet published (2022), the entry is labelled as company records and no stronger claim is made. Company recognitions are always attributed to Amber Homes Real Estate, not to any individual.

Corrections: if any wording here differs from the underlying certificate, write to ambreen@amberhomes.ae and it will be corrected. Full source register on the Evidence page.

Recognition, clarified.

What recognition has Amber Homes received?

Amber Homes received Platinum recognition for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025 across the Black Onyx Awards associated with Meraas, Nakheel, Dubai Properties and the wider Dubai Holding real-estate ecosystem, plus recognitions from Dubai Holding Real Estate (2021), Nshama (2020) and Binghatti (2026).

Was Ambreen Qureshi featured by RAKBANK?

Yes. Her entrepreneurial journey was featured through RAKBANK’s “She Means Business” campaign, which highlights female entrepreneurship in the UAE.

Are these Ambreen Qureshi’s personal awards?

No — and this site does not claim that. The Platinum recognitions and developer awards were received by Amber Homes Real Estate as a company. Ambreen Qureshi leads the company’s operations as Founder and Managing Director; the RAKBANK “She Means Business” feature relates to her personally as an entrepreneur.
